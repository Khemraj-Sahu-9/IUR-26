# ASHA Domain Knowledge & Field Operations

## Purpose
Guide the development of field-level workflows for Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs) under the National Health Mission (NHM) of India. Ensures terminology, workflows, and responsibilities reflect authentic community healthcare practices.

## When to Use
Use whenever designing, reviewing, or implementing:
- Household surveys and demographic records
- Antenatal care (ANC) and postnatal care (PNC) tracking
- Immunization registers and child growth tracking
- Essential drug kits and medicine requisition workflows
- Referral linkages between community, Sub-Centres, PHCs, and CHCs

## Core Domain Rules
1. **Community-Centric Demographics**: ASHAs serve a designated village or urban ward (~1,000 population in rural areas). Households are identified by geographic clusters, house numbers, or family head names.
2. **Maternal Care Milestones**:
   - Early registration of pregnancy (<12 weeks).
   - Minimum 4 ANC checkups (1st trimester, 14-26 weeks, 28-34 weeks, 36 weeks to delivery).
   - Institutional delivery encouragement under JSY (Janani Suraksha Yojana).
   - PNC home visits on Days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 42 for home deliveries (Days 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42 for institutional deliveries).
3. **Child Health Tracking**:
   - Birth dose vaccines (BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B birth dose).
   - Primary immunization at 6, 10, 14 weeks, and 9-12 months.
   - Malnutrition screening (severe acute malnutrition SAM red flags).
4. **Drug Kit Management**:
   - ASHAs carry standard drug kits: Paracetamol, ORS packets, Zinc tablets, IFA (Iron Folic Acid), Cotrimoxazole, Albendazole, pregnancy test kits (Nishchay), condoms, and oral contraceptive pills.
   - Stock replenishments flow through monthly sector meetings or PHC requests.
5. **Referral Linkages**:
   - Red flag identification (high fever, severe bleeding, convulsions, infant lethargy) requires urgent referral with transport assistance (108/102 ambulances).

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] No clinical diagnoses generated by the app; only observation records and protocol-based alerts.
- [ ] Language uses familiar Indian public health terminology (ANC, PNC, IFA, ORS, Immunization, Sub-Centre, PHC).
- [ ] Workflows reflect actual physical registers (RCH register, visit diary).
